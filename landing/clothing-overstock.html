Frequently asked questions

How do I buy overstock and clearance stock from China?

Browse live lots on stock-clearance.ai, open a listing, and send an inquiry with your target quantity. Suppliers reply with pricing and shipping terms. All trade is wholesale B2B; there is no retail checkout.

What is the minimum order (MOQ)?

MOQ is shown on each listing and varies by lot. Because the goods are already produced, many surplus lots are available in single-pallet or single-carton quantities.

Do suppliers ship worldwide?

Yes. Suppliers arrange global shipping by sea or air freight; logistics are agreed directly between buyer and supplier.

Is stock-clearance.ai a retailer?

No. It is a B2B marketplace connecting global buyers with Chinese surplus-stock suppliers. It does not hold inventory or process payments.
